Sales Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on sales data using Python. The analysis focuses on understanding customer demographics, purchasing patterns, sales performance, and product trends.

The dataset is cleaned and analyzed using Pandas, followed by visual exploration with Matplotlib and Seaborn.

🎯 Objectives
Clean and prepare the sales dataset for analysis
Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior
Identify high-performing states based on orders and sales
Compare purchasing patterns by gender and age group
Analyze sales by marital status and occupation
Identify top-performing product categories
Find the most frequently ordered products
Derive insights from customer and sales patterns

 Key Findings
Female customers represent a larger share of buyers and purchasing amount.
Customers in the 26–35 age group account for a significant share of purchases.
Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka are among the leading states by orders and sales.
Married women show strong purchasing activity.
Customers working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation contribute significantly to purchases.
Food, Clothing, and Electronics are among the leading product categories.
